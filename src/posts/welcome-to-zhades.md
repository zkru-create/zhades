## hey, it's Z ✦

Okay, deep breath. This is post number one, and honestly I've rewritten this first line about ten times.

Welcome to **Zhades**. The name is just me plus *shades*: Z + shades. Because that's what this place is. Every shade of what I love, all in one corner of the internet.

## so, who am I?

I'm Z. Gamer, painter, storyteller, self stylist and content creator, running on chai and creativity from Bengaluru.

Some days I'm deep in a game and "one more round" turns into three hours. Some days I've got paint on my hands and on my sleeves and somehow on my phone. And most days there's a story playing in my head that I haven't written down yet.

I've never been good at picking just one thing. So I stopped trying.

## what you'll find here

Zhades has four corners. Wander into whichever one matches your mood:

- **Shade Stories:** outfits, colour palettes and things that just *look* good.
- **Tinted Worlds:** the games I'm playing, loving and rage-quitting.
- **Colour Diaries:** stories I write, stories I tell, and little bits of my own life.
- **Splash Lab:** behind the scenes of making stuff, from sketches to videos to how I plan it all.

There's also a gallery where my paintings get to live outside my sketchbook for once.

## why start a blog now?

Because my camera roll is mostly half-finished paintings and screenshots of games, and my notes app is full of stories with no endings. They deserve better than that.

I wanted one place where all of it fits together, with no rules and no single aesthetic. Fashion one week, a game the next, a short story after that. If it has colour and a story behind it, it belongs here.

The plan is a new post every week. Next up: the story of a painting I almost threw away, and why I'm glad I didn't.

## come say hi

If you've read this far, you're officially one of the first people here, and that means a lot.

Tell me what you want to see more of, a game I should play, or a colour you can't stop wearing. [Say hi here](/contact/).

Pull up a chair. Let's make it colourful. ✦

**– Z**
